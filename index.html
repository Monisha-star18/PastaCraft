Types of pasta

Six shapes worth knowing
before you start.

Each shape below is built for a different job on the plate. Scroll the shelf
and take a jar down to read what it's for.

🍜

Tagliatelle

Long, flat ribbons cut for rich, clingy sauces like ragù.

🌀

Fusilli

Long, flat ribbons cut for rich, clingy sauces like ragù.

🎀

Farfalle

Pinched "bow-tie" pasta, sturdy enough for cream sauces.

👂

Orecchiette

Little "ears" that cup broccoli rabe and crumbled sausage.

🥟

Ravioli

Filled pockets — your first shape that hides an ingredient inside.

🐚

Cavatelli

Small rolled shells, dense enough to hold their bite in soup.
